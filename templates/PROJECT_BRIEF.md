These outcomes, the intended users, and the excluded scope below form the project's **compass**. Reuse an existing brief or North Star rather than create a second authority. A new batch must name the outcome it advances or the demonstrated blocker it removes. Define the evidence and stopping condition for that batch before working; do not expand the finish line merely because more checks are possible.

Stakeholder intent and field experience can justify an explicit engineering rule without a supporting experiment. Label that basis honestly; keep measured research findings, assumptions, extrapolations, and chosen policy distinct. Change the compass only through its responsible authority, preserving what changed and why.
